### About the data

This [PIDD](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/7zcc8v6hvp/1) dataset, measuring from National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK) from US , is a medical dataset investigate female patients in pima Indian region(which is the **highest incidence region in type 2 diabetes around the world**) to predict the occurence of diabates. This dataset records the female of above 21 years old , including **9 features factors**.

Based on the Visualisation, we can provide the insight on which factors contributed a lot in the risk of diabetes.
